### **Information**
My favorite movie "series", if you could call it that, is the Dracula movie series, which is technically not a proper series of films since movie directors across our modern history has interpreted Bram Stoker's Dracula novel in their own ways. Dracula is a story I find compelling for several reasons:

1. Its timeless nature allowed it to transcend its late Victorian era debut and become a long-lasting influence on literature and pop culture
2. Classic "good versus evil" tale.
3. The suspense and tension enhanced by the juxtaposition between 19th century technology and progress versus proven superstition and religious faith.
4. Themes like Victorian sexual purity, vague analogues to LGBT rights in late Victorian era England's draconian laws, and the fear of the "counter-colonizer" who will invade England and (metaphorically) drain the land's lifeblood.
5. Renowned actors like Bela Lugosi and Sir Christopher Lee brought their unique take on the titular Count, becoming pop culture icons in their own right.
